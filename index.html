    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the dedicated FTE model?

    You get full-time professionals who work only on your account during agreed hours. CollabRev employs them and handles HR, IT and compliance. You direct their day-to-day work.

    Do we need both FP&A and RCM services?

    No. Many clients start with one role, such as a staff accountant or AR specialist, and add more over time. With both, the dashboards connect collections directly to the P&L.

    Are the AI-assisted dashboards included?

    Yes, access is included with every engagement. Your team keeps the data current and reviews every figure. The AI assistant explains the numbers but never changes them.

    Can you support a DSO that is still acquiring practices?

    Yes. New locations adopt the same procedures and appear in the same dashboards, and we add professionals as your volume grows.

    How do you protect patient and financial data?

    Access is role-based, staff sign confidentiality agreements, workstations are secured, and RCM workflows are designed with HIPAA in mind. We review our full security setup with you during the consultation.
